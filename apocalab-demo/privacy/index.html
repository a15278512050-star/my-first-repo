Privacy in this demonstration

The prototype does not require an account and does not implement its own tracking analytics or data collection endpoint. These statements describe this demonstration's application code, not the practices of third-party hosts.

Map and third-party requests

The browser loads open-source map software through a public CDN and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. The deployment host and these service providers may process routine request information such as IP addresses and user-agent data according to their own privacy policies.

Place selections and URL sharing

Your selected latitude, longitude and zoom are handled in the browser. They appear in the URL only when you actively choose the Share action. If you share that URL, recipients and network services handling that link may see the embedded location.

Storage and cookies

This demonstration does not intentionally create accounts, set analytics cookies, or save a database of selected points. The hosting/CDN providers may apply their own technical cookies or logs. For publication as a consumer product, the operator must review this page and add accurate legal contact details.
