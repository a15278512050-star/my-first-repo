Fallout map: understanding wind without guessing deposition

Wind matters when studying airborne transport, but a single compass direction cannot provide a location-specific 24-hour deposition footprint. This page offers an interactive orientation diagram, not a predicted fallout map.

Not a forecast: The arrows below indicate compass direction only. They do not depict plume footprints, radioactive contamination, or risk to people.

Selected direction: East. Orientation only, no weather data.

Why direction is not enough

Atmospheric transport changes with winds at different altitudes, atmospheric stability, precipitation, terrain, and observation time. Rigorous forecasting requires verified time-dependent meteorological inputs and specialized science—not an arbitrary rotating shape on a map.
