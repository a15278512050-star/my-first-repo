Nuclear bomb map: an honest geographic explainer

A geographic map can clarify locations and distances, but it does not automatically make an effects prediction credible. The ApocaLab educational viewer separates measured geographic quantities from artistic animation.

What the interactive viewer displays

Choose a city to navigate, click a position to select the precise geographic point, and explore fixed-distance guide circles. The numerical panel provides WGS84 coordinates, a Leaflet map zoom level and labeled reference distances.

How the animation behaves

One click sets the point; pressing Play locks it for the entire artistic 28-second sequence. Pan and zoom remain possible, and the animated cloud stays attached to its original geographic coordinate. The effect is not a physical model.

What the viewer does not claim

It does not estimate nuclear blast boundaries, fallout, thermal injury zones, population effects or casualties. It does not provide weather forecasting, emergency response instructions or scientific hazard maps.
