How this site works: data, methods and missing pieces

This is an engineering demonstration, not a weapon-effects or emergency-planning calculator. Every number visible in the map interface has a simple, verifiable geographic meaning.

Reproducible geographic arithmetic

A circle has area πr² and perimeter 2πr. The 3 km reference circle has a mathematical area of 28.27 km², and the 5 km guide has a perimeter of 31.42 km (rounded to two decimal places). These are real mathematical measurements of geographic guide circles.

What is not calculated

This site does not implement nuclear weapon yield scaling, blast or thermal damage distances, fallout dispersion, individual exposure or population harm. It also does not load WorldPop population rasters: therefore, it does not display affected-population numbers.

Geographic and map data

Map tiles are requested at runtime from OpenStreetMap, with attribution displayed in the map. They are intended here for a limited evaluation, not high-traffic production. Place search is a small list of 20 preselected world cities included in JavaScript; the app makes no third-party geocoding calls. Map selection works anywhere tiles are available.

OSM data is under the Open Database License. Public OSM tile servers have a separate fair-use policy. Any production deployment must replace this demonstration source with a suitable licensed/hosted tile package.

Visual consistency during map zoom

The cloud is drawn on a transparent canvas whose position is projected from a fixed latitude and longitude. After Play, its anchor is immutable until the sequence ends. Its on-screen size changes with map zoom using a relative visual zoom factor; this is artistic alignment, not a claimed physical diameter or region of risk.

Precision and limitations

Coordinate formatting shows five decimal places, but those digits should not be interpreted as geodetic accuracy or a survey guarantee. Map labels and tiles may change. The reference circles denote nominal geographic scale, while the underlying projection distorts on-screen appearance. The rendering depends on browser support, network access and the remote tile service.

Acceptance tests still open

The original proposed weapon-effects calculator, data-backed effect tables, geographic fallout footprints, affected-population estimates and city-specific impact pages are not implemented. The dynamic social preview image for arbitrary coordinates is also unsupported on a pure static host: one generic OG image is used.
